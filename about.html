Madisco Ventures

Madisco Ventures is a personal investment and advisory practice based in San Francisco. We back pre-Series A founders working on hard problems in tech, data, AI, and food & beverage.

We don't run a fund-of-funds playbook. We write checks we'll wear on our sleeve, and when it's useful, we install senior operators — in technology, media, and revenue — directly into the companies we work with.

Hands-on, not heavy-handed. Curious about what you're building? Get in touch.
